FAQ

Everything you need to know before getting started. Can't find your answer? Just contact us.

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners. Whether you've never opened a Quran or you already know the basics, we will start from wherever you are. Our teachers are experienced in teaching all levels.

Yes, 100% free. No hidden fees, no credit card required. We believe you should try before you commit. If you are happy with the lesson, you can enroll. If not, there is absolutely no obligation.

Lessons take place via Zoom, Google Meet or another video platform of your choice. You will need a device with a camera and microphone. We send you a link before each lesson. No special software needed.

Standard lessons are 60 minutes. Children's lessons are 45 minutes, which is more suitable for young learners to stay focused. Lesson length can be discussed based on your needs and level.

We match you with the most suitable teacher based on your level, goals and course. During the free trial you will meet your teacher and if you feel the match is not right, we will find someone who is a better fit. Your comfort matters.

We teach students of all ages — from children age 5 to adults of any age. We have dedicated children's teachers for younger students and separate adult teachers. No one is too old or too young to start.

Very flexible. Lessons are available 7 days a week, mornings to evenings. We accommodate students from different time zones. You and your teacher will agree on a regular fixed time that works for both of you.

We ask that you inform us at least 24 hours in advance. Lessons cancelled with proper notice can be rescheduled at no extra cost. We understand that life happens — we are flexible and human about this.

Our standard rate is €20 per lesson (60 min) or €70 per month for 4 lessons. Children's lessons are €15 per lesson (45 min). Prices are indicative — we discuss the exact fee during your free trial. The first lesson is always free.
