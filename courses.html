Absolute beginnersAll ages

  • Short Surahs and basic recitation

Tajweed

Master the rules of Quranic recitation. Learn how every letter should sound, where to pause, how to apply Ghunna, Madd, Idghaam and all other Tajweed rules step by step.

IntermediateCan already read
  • Makharij al-Huroof (articulation points)
  • Sifaat (letter characteristics)
  • Madd, Ghunna, Idghaam, Iqlab
  • Waqf (pausing) and Ibtida
