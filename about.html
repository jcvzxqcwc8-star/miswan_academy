Our mission

Every Journey Begins Here

Whether you are a Hafiz, just starting to read, or somewhere in between — you are welcome here. We have the right teachers, the right program, and a community that supports you every step of the way.

At Miswan Quran Academy, it doesn't matter where you start. We will teach you according to your level — step by step, with patience and care. Insha Allah.

Our teachers are qualified Huffaz who hold Ijazah in Quranic recitation. They are patient, experienced and genuinely care about your progress. No matter your background or starting point — you belong here.
